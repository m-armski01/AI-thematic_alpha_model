# thematic-alpha

A macro-gated thematic equity **strategy research engine**. It formalizes a discretionary,
macro-gated momentum process over an AI-infrastructure basket into a reproducible, testable
system: data layer with a cross-exchange calendar and FX decomposition, a point-in-time feature
panel, a rules-based strategy (macro gate, momentum ranker, earnings mask, sizing limits), a
custom cost-aware backtester, a risk module, and a generated report.

**What it found (Layer 1, data through 2026-09-11):** the rules did *not* beat simply holding
the basket. Net of costs the strategy compounded at 37.1%/yr with a Sharpe of 1.07 and a −50.8%
maximum drawdown, versus 43.3%/yr, 1.24 and −46.7% for an equal-weight buy-and-hold of the same
twelve names. Both crush the S&P 500 (11.8%/yr) — but that gap is almost entirely the
hindsight-selected universe, not the rules. See [Limitations](#limitations-read-these-first).

> **This is a research and analysis system, not a live trading system.** It never places
> orders, integrates with a broker, or paper-trades. Historical results do not indicate future
> performance. Nothing here is investment advice.

## Limitations (read these first)

1. **Hindsight bias in universe selection — the dominant effect.** The twelve names were
   chosen in 2026 knowing they had been the AI-infrastructure winners. Quantified: holding
   them equal-weight returned ~75× since 2015 against ~3.8× for the S&P 500. None of that
   ~20× gap was available ex-ante; it is selection, not skill. Absolute returns in this
   repository should be read as *upper bounds on a lucky basket*, and every claim about the
   rules is made relative to that basket. A neutral-universe run over sector ETFs (Layer 3)
   is the planned control.
2. **Short window, one dominant regime.** Eleven years that were mostly a secular bull market
   in exactly this theme, with three drawdowns (2018, 2020, 2022) and none lasting more than
   ~19 months. Sharpe ratios above 1 on a concentrated long-only basket are a regime
   statement, not a strategy statement.
3. **Unhedged FX.** Base currency is EUR; positions are USD and KRW. The FX contribution is
   reported separately (+0.9%/yr of CAGR over this window) and is not a repeatable source of
   return.
4. **Survivorship in benchmarks.** Index ETFs are survivorship-biased by construction; not
   corrected.
5. **Macro data is current-vintage.** FRED series are revised; Layer 1 uses today's vintage
   with a one-session publication lag as the only point-in-time control. ALFRED vintages are a
   Layer 3 item.
6. **Earnings dates come from yfinance** and are reviewed by hand; where a ticker has none the
   mask *fails open* and says so in the log and the report.
7. **House-money sizing rule is not applied yet** (Layer 2); the config flag is accepted and
   announced, nothing else.

## Methodology

The strategy, every parameter of which lives in [`configs/base.yaml`](configs/base.yaml):

| Component | Rule |
|---|---|
| Universe | 12 AI-infrastructure names across compute, memory, hyperscalers, neocloud, optical, hardware (`data/reference/universe.csv`), plus `^GSPC`, `QQQ`, `SOXX` as benchmarks |
| Eligibility | A name enters the cross-section only after 252 observed sessions (no imputation; NBIS is first eligible 2025-10-22, IREN 2022-11-16) |
| Ranker | Cross-sectional z-score of 63-day momentum (skipping the last 5 days), top 5, conviction-tier weights 30/25/20/15/10% renormalized over survivors |
| Macro gate | Exposure ×0.5 when VIX > 25, ×0.7 when the 10y yield rose > 40 bp in 21 days, ×0.85 when WTI rose > 20% in 21 days; multiplicative, floored at 0.30. Macro inputs are lagged one session |
| Earnings mask | No *increase* in a name's weight within 3 sessions before a known earnings date; freed budget goes pro-rata to unblocked names; unknown dates fail open |
| Sizing | 35% per-name cap (excess to cash), 0% cash floor |
| Schedule | Signal on the last NYSE session of each week on or before Friday; executed the next session at the open |
| Costs | 5 bp per side + 3 bp slippage on traded notional, paid from cash; buys are sized so cash never goes negative |
| Benchmarks | `^GSPC` buy-and-hold; equal-weight buy-and-hold of the universe (re-equalized only when a new name becomes eligible); naive equal-weight top-5 momentum with no gate or mask — all through the same engine and cost model |

**Point-in-time discipline.** Every feature at date *t* uses data through the close of *t*;
features are computed on each ticker's own exchange sessions and aligned onto a master calendar
that is the union of NYSE and KRX sessions, forward-filling only across a name's own holidays.
`tests/test_no_lookahead.py` asserts (a) shifting all inputs by one day moves the outputs with
them, (b) deleting all data after *t* leaves every value at or before *t* unchanged, (c) a
synthetic future price spike leaves no trace before it, and (d) a price shock after *t + 1*
cannot alter the trade produced by a signal at *t*.

**Ablation and factor attribution** (which rule contributes what; whether the alpha survives
sector and momentum exposure) are Layer 2 deliverables and not yet built.

## What didn't work

- **The rules underperform holding the basket on every headline metric.** Lower CAGR (37.1%
  vs 43.3%), lower Sharpe (1.07 vs 1.24), deeper maximum drawdown (−50.8% vs −46.7%). Weekly
  top-5 rotation turned the book over 18× a year and paid 2.6% of final equity in costs; the
  naive momentum baseline (no gate, no mask) also beat the full strategy, so the gate and the
  mask together *cost* return over this window rather than adding it.
- **The macro gate de-risked into the recoveries.** It cut exposure to 68% on average through
  2020 and 2022, but it is a level rule on lagged VIX and it stayed risk-off well into the
  rebounds (see `outputs/figures/gate_vs_vix.png`). Whether it improves anything on a
  risk-adjusted basis is exactly what the Layer 2 ablation is for.
- **Normal VaR understates the tail.** Daily excess kurtosis is 3.7; parametric 99% VaR is
  4.6% against a historical 5.8%.

## Known data problems and how they are handled

| Problem | Handling |
|---|---|
| Hindsight / selection bias | Stated first; equal-weight buy-and-hold of the same basket reported everywhere as the reference; neutral universe planned for Layer 3 |
| Short histories (NBIS, IREN) | No imputation. A name is excluded from the cross-section until it has 252 sessions; weights renormalize over survivors (`tests/test_universe.py`) |
| Regime break (IREN) | `regime_start_date` column in `universe.csv` truncates history; left blank by default |
| SK Hynix adjusted close negative through 2002 | yfinance mis-adjusts the Dec-2002 reverse split; `regime_start_date = 2003-01-02` truncates it (data quality report flags any `adj close <= 0`) |
| Cross-exchange calendars (KRX vs NYSE) | `exchange_calendars` sessions; master calendar = union; forward-fill only across a name's own holidays, never across a real data gap; zero-volume phantom bars on Korean holidays dropped |
| FRED gaps and revisions | Forward-fill only, never back-fill or interpolate; one-session publication lag before any signal sees a value; current vintage, stated |
| WTI negative print (2020-04-20) | The 21-day change floors its *denominator* at $1 so a percentage through a negative base is not produced |
| Earnings dates | Seeded from yfinance by `scripts/seed_earnings_dates.py`, reviewed by hand, version-controlled; missing tickers fail open with a warning |
| Ticker continuity (COHR) | yfinance series is continuous through the 2022 merger; 4 single-day moves > 25% flagged for review in `outputs/data_quality.md` |

## Reproduction

Requires Python 3.11+ and network access for the first run (yfinance + FRED; everything is
cached to Parquet under `data/cache/`).

```bash
git clone <this repo> && cd thematic_alpha_model
python3.12 -m venv .venv && source .venv/bin/activate
pip install -r requirements.txt && pip install -e .

# Full Layer 1 pipeline: data -> features -> strategy -> backtests -> risk -> report
python -m thematic_alpha.run --config configs/base.yaml

# Force a fresh download (ignores the cache)
python -m thematic_alpha.run --config configs/base.yaml --refresh

# Layer 0 smoke test (NVDA only)
python -m thematic_alpha.run --config configs/base.yaml --layer 0

# Offline test suite (no network) with coverage
pytest --cov=src/thematic_alpha
```

Outputs: `outputs/report.md`, `outputs/data_quality.md`, `outputs/figures/*.png`,
`outputs/feature_panel.parquet`, `outputs/target_weights.csv`. The report is deterministic:
identical inputs produce a byte-identical file. Headline numbers above are from the data
snapshot of 2026-09-11; a later run will extend the window and move them.

To refresh earnings dates: `python scripts/seed_earnings_dates.py`, then review the printed
spot-check list against the companies' investor-relations calendars before committing
`data/reference/earnings_dates.csv`.

```

data/reference/                   universe.csv, earnings_dates.csv (version-controlled)
data/cache/                       Parquet cache (gitignored)
src/thematic_alpha/
  config.py                       pydantic v2 schema, extra keys rejected
  data/        prices, macro (FRED CSV endpoint), calendar, fx, universe, quality, cache
  features/    price_features, macro_features, build
  strategy/    macro_gate, ranker, event_mask, sizing, compose
  backtest/    schedule, engine, costs, benchmarks
  risk/        metrics, drawdown
  reporting/   plots, tables, report
  pipeline.py                     one function per stage
  run.py                          CLI
scripts/seed_earnings_dates.py
tests/                            offline; synthetic two-exchange market in tests/synthetic.py
outputs/                          report.md, data_quality.md, figures/ (committed)
```

## Disclaimer

This project is a research and educational exercise in quantitative strategy design,
backtesting methodology, and machine-learning validation. It does not execute trades, does not
constitute investment advice, and its historical results do not indicate future performance.
The universe was selected with hindsight and results are correspondingly biased upward.
